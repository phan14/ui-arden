## Intent findings

- Non-branded commercial/service demand dominates: xưởng may, gia công, theo yêu cầu, số lượng ít, TPHCM and thiết kế.
- Informational demand includes material/product concepts, factory imagery and industry/trend topics.
- Twenty query rows contain Arden-like brand terms, totaling 0 clicks / 221 impressions in the exported aggregate table. Brand visibility exists but branded click evidence is weak in this export.
- Several high-ranking zero-click image/factory queries appear at positions 1–10. Search appearance/export context is insufficient to treat these as conversion traffic.
- Competitor-like `gocy` queries have large impressions. They are not evidence to insert competitor terms into content.

## Content implication

Preserve the current successful service/article URLs and improve title, description, H1 alignment and content depth in place. Do not create duplicate new routes targeting the same “xưởng may/gia công/theo yêu cầu” intent without a cannibalization map.
